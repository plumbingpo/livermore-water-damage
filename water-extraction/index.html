Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Across Livermore, Livermore Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
